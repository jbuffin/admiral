# Chat questions, ship cards and drafts

Chat questions, ship cards, report turns and replies, drafting ships and campaign drafts. Read it before changing `internal/chat` cards or report turns.

- **Chat questions** (`internal/chat/questions.go`, ADR 0023).
  - The chat's `ask_question` MCP tool posts to `POST /api/chat/questions`, which takes the fleet from the actor token (never an argument) and writes `$ADMIRAL_HOME/chat/<fleet>/questions/<id>.json` (the question item); the tool then waits on `GET /api/chat/questions/<id>/answer`, which reads `<id>.answer.json`.
  - The service reads the directory through an `os.Root` (no links, `O_NONBLOCK`, at most 1 MiB, the id rule, `inbox.ReadItem` with kind `question` only; a file that fails is skipped), on the chat's 500 ms tick (while a browser is connected) and at most every 100 ms; `GET /api/chat/session` and a new connection read it fresh.
  - It runs whether or not the chat runs, outside `s.mu`, newest names first until 20 valid questions are found, and a file whose modification time and size haven't changed is not read again.
  - `questions` holds the newest 20, oldest first, each the inbox's question item with `session` `_chat-<fleet>` and `answer`: the answer file's content (read just as defensively and decoded into `answers.<id>.choice`/`other`, `note`, `answered` and the envelope, so nothing else a writer put there is served; a link, an oversized file, or one that doesn't decode is no answer) or `null`.
  - The answer route builds the file with `inbox.BuildQuestionAnswer` and writes it with `inbox.WriteAnswerFile` under the fleet chat's own mutex.
  - One fleet's chat never reads, answers or withdraws another's question.
  - Chat questions never touch `inbox/`, `GET /api/inbox` or the `inbox` message.
  - New chat, and a fresh session started after the old one ended (just before the launcher creates it, `Launcher.BeforeChat`), remove every file in that fleet's `chat/<fleet>/questions/`; a waiting `ask_question` then gets no answer.
- **Ship cards** (`internal/chat/shipcard.go`, `cmd/admiral/shipcards_wire.go`).
  - `ShipCards.Observe` takes the ship snapshot (`ships.Service.List`) and the outcome log's records of the last 14 days, and posts one `ship_card` per voyage (session + `FirstLaunched`) and result through `PostShipCard`:
  - `moored` (first moor), `anchored`.
    - `failed` (a scout whose `ended` record carries `result: "failed"`: posted in place of `sailed_off` on the same triggers; `ship-cards.json` without `Scouts` settles it silently too).
    - `done` (a local-only ship's `finished` record, or a clean `ended` whose report result is `done` where `sailed_off` would post; one slot with `sailed_off` and `failed`; the card and its report turn carry the ship's `branch`, `PostedShipCards` and the digest read it, and a ship with no worktree has none).
    - `sailed_off` (a clean `ended` with no `moored` after it, `ShipSettleGrace` later and once the voyage has left the snapshot, since a ship stays `sailing_off` there while settle runs; or an `unmoored` for merged, closed or prs_done that no `returning` follows within `ShipSettleGrace`, which is how a return to port's `unmoored` and `returning` are read together; or a `stopped` with `scout: true`, which a scout writes after its killed `ended`, once the voyage has left the snapshot and `ShipSettleGrace` has passed; a `returned` ship never sails off).
  - The server decides a card's `skill_kind`: `scout` for a scout, then `commodore`, then `quick` (AdmiralBot, no skill), else the ship's kind with an empty one read as `skill` or `none`.
  - `ship-cards.json` carries `Scouts`; a file saved without it settles every scout stop already logged silently, as an older build read it as a plain stop, `returned` (a `stopped` with `returned`) and, for `Via: admiralbot` ships, `ready_to_merge`.
  - It keeps the voyages and a first-pass seed in `$ADMIRAL_HOME/ship-cards.json`, so a restart posts nothing twice and ships already moored or anchored at the first run get no card, and a ship moored then gets no `ready_to_merge` for its first answered poll; a voyage with no parsable launch stamp gets none; the chat log's ID check is the second guard.
  - The runner starts after the host's first listing, passes on each ship change and every 5 s, and posts no push or turn.
  - For a scout, an engineer's Stop (a `stopped` record without `scout`, once the voyage left the snapshot and `ShipSettleGrace` passed) and a loss (gone from the snapshot with no end record for `ShipSettleGrace`, never counted while `Host.Snapshot` reports tmux lost or `Host.Unreadable` names the ship: `ShipCards.HostState`) post a card as well, `failed` unless `ShipCards.KeptFindings` (wired to `Service.KeptFindings`) finds findings, then `sailed_off`; a non-scout ship's Stop and loss post nothing yet.
  - `ship-cards.json` carries `Groups`: a file saved without it marks every scout card already posted as reported and every unsettled scout gone from the snapshot as settled, so the first pass posts no new card or turn for them.
  - When the chat is served, `fleetChat.recent` adds `findings: true` to a card whose voyage `findings.Reader.Has` finds (a stat, never logged); the web's `FindingsSheet` fetches and renders them.
- **Report turns** (`internal/chat/shipreport.go`).
  - The detector's `QueueReports` hook, wired in `cmd/admiral/main.go`, calls `Service.EnqueueShipReport` once per reporting event, before the card posts and before `Done[result]` is set, so a failed enqueue is retried by the next pass; the card id is the queue item id.
  - A voyage reports when `Via == admiralbot`, it has no `Commitment` or `Routine` key, it is not a scout (a scout reports through its group, below) and its `Round` is 0 (feedback rounds, wakes and hand relaunches keep the voyage; `Round` comes from the session option or `moor.Record.Round`).
  - Only the first of `anchored`, `moored` or a `sailed_off` with no moor reports; Ready to merge, returned, the end after a moor and seeding post the card only.
  - `ReportTurnText` writes `[Report: <name>] …` with the card id, ship, first launch, kind, result, the Admiral report's result (from the `ended` outcome record's `report`, since a moored ship's report file is deleted right after the moor; the live file is the fallback and `unknown` means no report was written), PRs and a `findings:` line (`kept, read them with read_findings, passing ship and first_launched` if `Service.Findings.Has` finds findings for the card's voyage as the turn is built, else `none`; a kept line adds a sentence telling AdmiralBot to say so, that the card opens them, and never to paste them whole); the title and launch text (from the launch-line entry, else the title) sit in backtick fences, cleaned of control, bidi and line-separator characters first.
  - The done-only-if-done-and-a-PR rule is in that text and in `chatPromptTemplate`.
- **Scout group report turns** (`internal/chat/scoutgroup.go`).
  - A scout AdmiralBot launches carries the session option `prompt-key` (`Ship.PromptKey`, set from `ships.Service.PromptKey`, which is `chat.Service.PromptKey`: the engineer's chat turn, `<nonce>-<prompt stamp>`; unlike `ChatPrompt` it keys no hold, so an `anyway` launch records it too), which the detector keeps on the voyage as first seen, so a rewritten option moves no scout between groups.
  - After settling every voyage, `reportScoutGroups` groups the voyages that are scouts with `Via == admiralbot`, no `Commitment` or `Routine` key, `Round` 0 and not yet `Reported` by fleet and key; a scout with no key is a group of one.
  - A group reports once every member's end card has posted (`sailed_off`, `failed` or `anchored`) and `TurnOver(fleet, key)` says its engineer turn is over (`Service.TurnOver`: the chat's prompt key moved, the chat is idle or its session is gone; a key that can't be read while the chat runs waits for the next pass; a keyless scout needs no such check).
  - It calls the `QueueScoutReports` hook (`Service.EnqueueScoutReport`) with the card of the scout whose end came last (`voyage.ScoutEnd`, ties by session name) and every member's card, and marks the members `Reported` only when that returns nil, so a failed enqueue is tried again. The queue id is the trigger card's id.
  - `ScoutGroupTurnText` writes one `[Report: …]` marker, one `card:` line and a part for each scout (ship, name, first launch, kind, result, `report result: none`, findings line, fenced title and launch text), then the rule that a scout is done only when it sailed off and kept findings; each scout's title and launch text share the budget and shrink, then drop, until the turn is under `sessionhost.MaxPaste`.
  - A quick or Commodore ship never joins or holds a group and keeps its own report turn.
- **Report replies** (`internal/chat/thread.go`).
  - `record` marks log entries with `thread: <ship card id>` through `threadState`: the user line matching the pasted turn, then the assistant entries until the engineer's message (during the turn) or any user line, a notice included (after `endTurn` marked it ended).
  - The pasted line is matched on its `card: <id>` line and the paste time; a restart between paste and record marks a delivered turn's line with no thread yet, and pushes nothing.
  - A fleet digest turn works the same way, matched on the `turn: digest:<routine>:<run>` line its text carries right after the `[Digest: <name>]` marker; its first marked reply goes to `Service.OnDigestReply`, which `cmd/admiral/main.go` turns into `push.DigestReplyEvent` (kind `digest_reply`, key `digest-reply-<turn id>`, target `chat`, no ship card and no Inbox item).
  - The shell nests a marked message only under a ship card with that id, so a digest reply stays top-level and its typed turn shows collapsed like a report turn. Marks are set at first append.
  - `trackTurn` calls `endTurn(id, how)` once per end; a normal end forces a transcript read and hands the first marked assistant entry to `Service.OnReply` once (`reported` in turns.json), which `cmd/admiral/main.go` turns into `push.ReportReplyEvent` (kind `report_reply`, target `chat`).
  - A dropped turn, or a reply logged after the end, pushes nothing.
  - The client folds marked entries under their ship card in `threadOf` (`web/src/app/chat.ts`) and opens `/app/chat/<fleet>/message/<id>` at the message.
- **Drafting ships follow their campaign** (`internal/ships/drafting.go`, `internal/inbox/drafting_ended.go`).
  - `Service.drafters` holds one in-memory `draftState` per drafting ship, keyed by session and first launch, with the launch's fleet and harness (never the pane's options, which a ship can rewrite), so an Admiral restart loses it.
  - `advanceDrafting` reads the ship's `campaign-ref` at each turn end, anchoring and sail-off and calls `Service.FollowDrafted`, which `cmd/admiral/main.go` sets to `follow.Service.FollowDrafted` so `ships` never imports `follow`.
  - `serveStop`, `serveDismiss` and `settle` call `captureDraftEnd` while the pane and folder still exist, then follow the last ref and post the `drafting_ended` item through `inbox.WriteDraftingEnded`.
  - That item's conversation route reads through `shipconv.Service.RecordedConversation`, with the ADR 0019 checks. Fields and rules: `docs/api.md`.
- **AdmiralBot's drafting ship.** `launch_ship` with `drafting` sends `drafting: true` and `autopilot: false` whatever `with_me` says, and refuses without a skill, with `scout`, `clone`, `source`/`order_id` or `issues` before any call; the server's own refusals (skill installed nowhere, ambiguous) pass through.
  - `get_drafting_skill` reads the fleet's drafting skill with the repo's `dir`.
  - The brief tells AdmiralBot to draft only when told, to launch a drafting ship with the fleet's skill, or the one the engineer names, and to use `draft_campaign` only for a quick draft, and never to change the skill.
  - A chat launch (`req.OnInstruction`) marks its `draftState` with `fromChat`; when `applyRef` follows its ref it posts a `following_card` through `Service.PostFollowingCard` (wired to `chat.Service.PostFollowingCard`), naming the campaign from what `Service.FollowDrafted` returns (its ID and the stored title Admiral fetched), so a transferred issue's URL still finds its card; the card's ID carries the ship's launch stamp and the server strips invisible characters from its title with `brief.Clean`. A modal launch posts none. Card shape: `docs/api.md`, "Following cards".
- **Campaign drafts** (`internal/inbox/campaign_draft.go`, `internal/chat/campaigndraftcard.go`).
  - AdmiralBot's `draft_campaign` posts to `POST /api/fleets/<fleet>/campaign-drafts`, which the inbox service serves: it writes a `campaign_draft` item and holds no gh client and no drafter, so it can't write to GitHub.
  - The repo must be in `Service.FleetRepos` (the fleet's projects that route to it, wired in `cmd/admiral/main.go`).
  - Create and Dismiss are the engineer's `/create` and `/dismiss` item actions; Create calls `follow.Service.CreateCampaign` (the core of `serveCreate`) through the `CreateCampaign` hook outside `answerMu`, one action per item at a time.
  - Once the parent exists the item is answered even if Follow failed.
  - Both post a `campaign_draft_card` with no turn; AdmiralBot is not told the outcome.
- **Kept out.** No outcomes beyond ship cards, autopilot, caps or resources; no crew rows.
- **Need cards** (`internal/chat/needcard.go`). Shape and route: `docs/api.md`, "Need cards".
  - `Service.RunNeedCards` takes a poke from `InboxChanged` (the Inbox's `OnChange` and the ships' change, fanned out in `cmd/admiral/main.go`) and posts a `need_card` for each listed question, pending review and findings item of a ship with a fleet, and each routine proposal, allow rule proposal and campaign draft of a configured fleet (`needKinds`; each kind's card fields come from `needCardBody`), on its own goroutine. The id hashes session, item id and `created`; the log skips an id it holds, so a restart posts nothing twice. The pass reserves the card's record in memory before it appends the entry, drops it if the append fails and saves it after, so a client that sees the card always finds its record and a crash between posts the card again.
  - `need-cards.json` under the home keeps one record per card (fleet, session, item, `created`, state, where answered). The entry in the log keeps the card as posted and `recent` serves its state from the record, settling it first when the item has left the Inbox's list (answer file: answered in the Inbox; none: removed).
  - `inbox.Service.OnSettled(session, id, removed, via)` runs after the answer file is written (from `Answer`, `AnswerGuarded` and `RemoveItem`), before a kept ship's files are released. The card route's `inbox.Guard` carries `Via: "chat"`, so only an answer the route actually wrote settles its card as `chat`; every other answer settles it `inbox`. While a tap is in flight, `needState` leaves the item's card open rather than guess.
  - The route answers through the item's kind (`needRec.Kind`; empty means question): `AnswerGuarded` for a question, `SubmitReviewGuarded` or `DiscardReviewGuarded` for a review, `AnswerFindingsGuarded` for findings. The `Guard`'s `Check` is a pure predicate that compares the item's `created` with the card's and the record's `sig` (a hash of the item's own field as posted), so a reused id or a changed item is refused; it runs before any gh call and again under the answer lock, and it marks nothing. `ForgetReview` (the Inbox's Remove) answers as removed, so the review's card reads removed.
  - The three fleet kinds (`fleetKinds`: `routine_proposal`, `allow_rules`, `campaign_draft`) live under `_admiral` and post in the chat of the fleet `needFleet` reads from the item (an allow rule item names it in its object, since the list leaves `Fleet` empty for an unconfigured fleet); their card has no `ship`. They answer through `LaunchRoutineItemGuarded`/`DismissRoutineProposalGuarded`, `AnswerAllowRulesGuarded` and `CreateCampaignItemGuarded`/`DismissCampaignDraftGuarded`, which check the Guard under the answer lock before any hook or write (campaign create has no lock around its hook, so it checks before the hook and again once it holds the item's one action) and call `OnSettled` with the Guard's `Via` after the answer file is written. `inbox.Refusal` gives the card route the Inbox's status and body for a refused launch or create. An unknown kind in the route fails with a 500, never as a question.
  - An allow rule card keeps no `sig`: the item grows in place under its id and `created`, so the card binds on those and `recent` serves the item's current rules (`refreshRules`).
  - `MovedOn` reads a routine proposal as closed without the engineer: its answer file says `expired`, or it has none and its expiry has passed (`Service.Now` is the clock, so a test drives expiry). `MovedOn` never takes `answerMu` (a prompt routine's launch hook reads the chat, which calls it): the Inbox marks a routine launch in flight from before it takes the lock until its answer file is written and `MovedOn` reports `launching`, which `needState` answers by leaving the card open, as for a chat tap; otherwise it settles `moved_on` before the answer-file cases, so an engineer's launch always reads answered, and the card route reads the record, not the clock, so a tap meeting an unsettled expired proposal gets the Inbox's 410 and one after the card moved gets 409.
  - Nothing settles from an item's absence before `Inbox.Scanned()`: the list is empty until the first `Refresh`. A `need-cards.json` that fails to parse is moved to `need-cards.json.bad` once, never overwritten.
  - **Stop cards** (`internal/chat/needstop.go`). The same pass walks `Service.Ships` (poked by `ShipsChanged`, wired to the ships' `OnChange`) and posts a card for each ship that needs you for `permission`, `stop` or `not_started`, reserved and saved as the Inbox cards are. The record's `Kind` is the stop kind (`stopKinds`), so the Inbox item settling code skips stop records.
  - A stop is bound by `ships.Ship.StopID`, the pane option `@admiral-stop-id` the hook writes when it sets `needs_you` (the `-o` re-write of an `ifUnset` Notification keeps the old id) and clears on `working`. `not_started` has no hook write, so its identity is the launch stamp. `@admiral-stop-answered` (`"<id>"` or `"<id> admiralbot"`) is set by `markStopAnswered` in `internal/ships/input.go` before a route sends, and undone when the send fails. It is never cleared: it names its stop, and the card reads it after the ship works.
  - `settleStop` reads the ship list: the same stop still needing you stays open, even with `StopAnswered` on it (an Escape at an idle prompt marks and ends nothing); once the stop has ended, `StopAnswered` on it is `answered` (`admiralbot` or `inbox`), a ship that left is `moved_on`, and anything else is `settled_terminal`. Stop ids and answered marks are read only when `sessionhost.ValidStopID` accepts them (16 lowercase hex, the shape `NewStopID` makes); anything else is no stop id.
  - The ship answer route's optional `stop_id` runs the same live-id and answered-mark check (`stopStillOpen`, shared with `AnswerStop`; 409 `already answered`) before its pane-state check, and it marks every answer, question menu included. An item card answered through the Inbox settles `AnsweredAdmiralBot` when the request carried the actor (`InboxSettled` reads the Guard's via).
  - `AnswerStop` refuses with `ErrStopMoved` when the live answered mark already names the card's stop: the first answer wins. A card the chat's own route is answering is skipped, so the poller never serves `settled_terminal` between a tap and the state change.
  - The route calls `Service.AnswerStop` (`ships.Service.AnswerStop`), which takes the pane's lock, re-reads the live state, reason and stop id, and returns `ErrStopMoved` (409), `*StopRefused` (400) or a plain error (500). Next-prompt text goes through `typeAtPrompt`, the same typing as `/next`.
  - The web half: `threadOf` turns a `need_card` entry into a `need` item, which keeps its own kind because a `question` item's state has `withdrawn`. `NeedCard` is a union on `item_kind`. The title is plain text, never markdown.
    - `NeedCardView` (`NeedCards.tsx`) shows a question with `PeekQuestions` and every other kind with the Inbox's own body (`ReviewBody`, `FindingsBody`, `RoutineProposalBody`, `AllowRulesBody`, `CampaignDraftBody`) through a chat `Acting`. The `Acting` maps a verb to the card route: `launch`, `dismiss` and `discard` become `{verb: true}`.
    - The body reads an `Entry` built from the card and the joined Inbox item (`inboxItems`, by session, `_admiral` for a fleet card, and id). A review or a set of finding cards with no match says "Details are in the Inbox". A settled card is a quiet line, with the ship's name only on a ship card.
    - `useChat.answerNeed` posts to the card route and keys the draft by the card id. It rereads after a 409, a 404, a campaign create that filed its parent and an allow rules 400 saying a card has no decision. It shows the server's message unless the reread left the card answered or the 400 was that one.
    - A stop card (`permission`, `stop`, `not_started`) shows Allow once and Deny, or a next-prompt text box and Send, through `StopCardBody`; one with no buttons says to answer in the terminal, and `not_started` points to the Terminal tab. A settled one reads Settled in the terminal, Moved on, or where it was answered, Answered via AdmiralBot among them.
  - **Reply in words.** An open card, except `not_started` and a terminal-only permission, has a `Reply to AdmiralBot` box (`ReplyBox` in `NeedCards.tsx`) beside its buttons. `useChat.replyNeed` posts `{text, need_card}` to the send route with its own per-card text, busy and error, apart from the composer and `drafts`. `fleetChat.needCardHeader` (`needcard.go`) answers 404 for an unknown or other-fleet card and 409 for one no longer open, else returns the `[Need card: …]` identity header, which `serveSend` puts before the text. The header holds the card's identity only, never ship text. The thread strips it (`NEED_HEADER` in `ChatPanel.tsx`). A reply is an engineer turn (ADR 0051).
  - A reply refused with 404 or 409 whose card the reread shows closed moves its words to the composer (after any text there, on a new line) with a note, since the card's box goes with the card; a card still open keeps the words and the error in its box.
